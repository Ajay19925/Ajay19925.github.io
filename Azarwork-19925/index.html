Our Story (Since 2002)

Mr. A. Ayubkhan

Chairperson · GANI Dental Lab – Madurai

Mr. A. Ayubkhan spent five years working at Cuspal Dental, Nagoya, Japan, where he gained extensive experience and expertise in ceramic fabrication.

In 2002, he returned to India and established the Madurai laboratory, combining traditional craftsmanship with contemporary materials, technology, and digital workflows.

Nagoya, Japan Ceramic Mastery • 5 Years International Training

Our Root (Since 1994)

Mr. A. Anwardeen

Founder & MD · GANI Dental Groups

Completed Dental Mechanic Course at Armed Forces Medical College (AFMC), Pune, and served 20 years in the Army Dental Corps, receiving a Gold Medal in 1989 from President of India, Mr. R. Venkataraman.

Founded GANI Dental Laboratory in 1994, gaining international training across Japan, Germany, France, UK, and Switzerland. Expanded GANI Dental Groups across Coimbatore, Madurai, and Trichy.

1989 Presidential Gold Medalist • AFMC Pune • Army Dental Corps
